Who We Are

We're not a property company. We're a data company that builds homes.

Amro Partners sits at the intersection of intelligent investing and real human impact. We use machine learning, real-time data, and deep market expertise to develop and manage residential communities across Europe — sustainably, thoughtfully, and at scale.

What drives us isn't just returns. It's the knowledge that the models we build and the decisions we make shape where people live, how cities grow, and how sustainably the built world evolves.

What We Stand For

The things that
make us, us.

These aren't words on a wall. They're how we make decisions, hire, and measure what matters.

01

Data as the edge

We've built proprietary machine learning capabilities that turn raw market signals into investment intelligence. Everyone here — whatever their role — is curious about what the data is saying.

02

Impact you can point to

Our work isn't abstract. It becomes buildings. Communities. Streets that exist because someone here made a good decision. That's the kind of accountability we embrace.

03

Small team, real ownership

We're not a corporation. There's no layer between your work and its outcome. You'll have genuine ownership, meaningful voice, and the trust that comes with both.

04

Sustainability, seriously

100% of our assets target BREEAM Outstanding. This isn't a checkbox — it's a constraint we design around, a standard we've never compromised, and a commitment our investors depend on.

05

Curious over certain

The best ideas at Amro come from people who ask uncomfortable questions. We'd rather have the right answer late than the wrong answer early — and we hire people who feel the same.

06

Europe as the playground

London, Madrid, Berlin, Amsterdam. Four markets, four cultures, one coherent approach. Working here means thinking across borders — not just with your colleagues, but in your models.
